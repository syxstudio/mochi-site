## Constraints

- **Inline CSS only** (one `<style>` block, no external stylesheets except maybe Google Fonts)
- **No JavaScript** required (it's a static page)
- **Mobile-responsive** at 375px width minimum
- **Fast load** (no large images unless they're optimized)
- **Accessible** (semantic HTML, alt text on images, sufficient contrast)
- **No "Coming soon" language anywhere** (Apple flags placeholder content)
- **No fake testimonials or made-up stats** (Apple's reviewer can sniff fakery)

---

## What to AVOID

- Mochi's soft pink aesthetic (this is the studio brand, not the product brand)
- Generic startup-y copy ("revolutionize" / "leverage" / "cutting-edge" — banned)
- Em dashes (use commas or periods instead)
- Marketing clichés ("we believe", "imagine if")
- Empty hero with no content below
- Carousel sliders / popups / cookie banners (slow + not needed)

---

## Style of writing

- Direct. Specific. No marketing jargon.
- Sentences vary in length. Mix short and long.
- Conversational but grown-up. Not chirpy.
- The reader should feel: this is a real studio run by a thoughtful person, not a content farm or AI-generated shell page.
